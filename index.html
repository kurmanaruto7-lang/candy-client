FAQ

The stuff people ask.

Will this get me banned?

No. Candy only changes performance and cosmetic settings that Minecraft already exposes — render distance, particles, framerate, graphics mode. There is no combat, movement, or X-ray functionality of any kind, so there's nothing for anti-cheats to flag.

Will it really give me 100+ FPS?

On most setups the booster adds a large chunk of frames by trimming the expensive stuff. But no mod can promise an exact number on every GPU — a very old laptop will gain less than a gaming rig. It gives you every frame your hardware has to give.

Do I need a special launcher?

Nope. Candy is a normal Fabric mod. Use the official Minecraft launcher (or your favourite one) with a Fabric profile and drop the jar in your mods folder. Your account and worlds are untouched.

Is it really free?

Yes, for the whole beta. No payment, no trial timer, no locked features. When v1.0 ships some extras may become paid, but beta users keep what they've got.

Does it work with other mods?

It plays nicely with most performance and cosmetic mods. If you already run Sodium, Candy's booster will still manage the vanilla options layered on top.

Which Minecraft versions?

Built for Fabric on modern versions (1.20+). More versions come as the beta grows — ask on Discord for a specific one.
